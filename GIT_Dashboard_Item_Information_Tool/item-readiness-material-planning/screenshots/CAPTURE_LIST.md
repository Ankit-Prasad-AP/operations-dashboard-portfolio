## Screenshot quality standard
- Use a clean browser window with no bookmarks, profile names, emails, local paths, or unrelated tabs visible.
- Capture at a desktop width of roughly 1440–1600 px.
- Keep the first screenshot focused on the decision story rather than a long technical table.
- Confirm every visible identifier is synthetic.
- Strip image metadata before publishing.

No screenshot is bundled in this revision because the build environment could not complete Chromium screenshot capture reliably. Capture the final public images locally after the browser verification step and before publishing them to the portfolio.
