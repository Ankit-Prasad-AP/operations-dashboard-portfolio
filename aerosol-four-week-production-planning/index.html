How the decision logic works

The public demo uses generalized thresholds, but preserves the planning concepts and priority structure.

1

Protect the locked week

The first production week is treated as exact and is never rebalanced by the system.

2

Honor planner overrides

Active manual item rows remain exact for the later weeks with no system intervention.

3

Net supply against demand

Available inventory and exact production are credited before new production is recommended.

4

Carry eligible prior demand

Only valid prior-period limited-run shortage stays eligible within the generalized carry window.

5

Protect customer orders

Open orders establish a coverage floor; timing conflicts with the exact week remain visible.

6

Make quantities manufacturable

System-generated quantities are rounded to generalized pallet and batch minimums.

7

Balance flexible demand

Eligible future normal demand can be pulled forward only after committed requirements are protected.

8

Review before scheduling

Timing and master-data exceptions stay visible before the plan moves into line-level scheduling.
