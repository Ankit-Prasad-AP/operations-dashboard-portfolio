How the Decision Engine Works
The operating logic, simplified for a public manufacturing demo.
Step 1Read the production planOnly non-zero production creates material demand.
Step 2Translate cases to componentsEach run is multiplied by synthetic quantity-per-case BOM usage.
Step 3Choose the inventory basisValidated physical can inventory can replace system inventory for that component.
Step 4Add dated inbound supplyA purchase order dated on the production day is treated as available before the run.
Step 5Allocate shared materialSame-day demand is allocated from the largest requirement to the smallest.
Step 6Find the earliest safe dateIf the planned date is short, later in-week purchase orders are evaluated.
Step 7Escalate genuine supply gapsIf known supply cannot cover the run, the tool flags a future/new-supply action.
Step 8Protect the decisionEditing Page 1 marks material analysis stale until it is rerun.
Portfolio demonstration using deterministic synthetic manufacturing data. No employer, customer, supplier, product, material, purchase-order, sales-order, or operational source data is included.
