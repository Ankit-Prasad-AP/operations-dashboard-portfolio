13-Week Production Readiness Planner

A recruiter-facing demonstration of a manufacturing planning system that converts forecast, inventory, exact planner inputs, protected builds, and operating minimums into a sequenced 13-week production plan.

Demo mode · synthetic data · nothing leaves your browser

What This Does

This tool answers a practical planning question: what must be produced first to finish committed forecast, and what can be moved earlier only after those requirements are protected?

It combines demand, on-hand inventory, exact locked production, manual planner overrides, protected prebuild quantities, limited-duration demand, pallet rules, and batch minimums. The weekly normalization target is deliberately secondary to forecast completion.

How It Works

Load demand, inventory, exact production, planner overrides, and item attributes.
Convert negative inventory to zero for planning and reduce current-period demand by sold-to-date.
Protect exact locked, manual, and prebuild quantities before any system-generated production.
Finish earlier-period forecast first; later demand cannot hide an uncovered earlier requirement.
Apply limited-duration carry rules only inside the configured eligibility horizon.
Round valid production to pallet and batch minimums, with low-volume requirements consolidated to one run.
Only after forecast is protected, move eligible normal production earlier to improve weekly balance.
Surface data-quality and planning exceptions so a planner knows what requires intervention.

Data Privacy

All data in this public demo is deterministic synthetic manufacturing data. Nothing is uploaded to a server. The demo runs entirely in the browser. Exact production parameters from the private operational tool were generalized for portfolio use.
